# Cooking Base Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Cooking Base is a personal cookbook app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## The short version

Cooking Base does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your recipes stay on
your device. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

## What is stored, and where

Your recipes, categories, and settings (such as light or dark theme) are saved in a database
on your device only. The developer cannot see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted
with it unless you made a backup.

## When the app uses the internet

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to import a recipe:

- **Web link:** the app downloads the page at the address you entered to read its recipe.
- **Google Doc link:** the app downloads the text of the document at the link you entered.
  The document must be shared as "Anyone with the link". The app does not sign in to Google
  and does not access your Google account.

These requests go directly from your device to that website or Google, like opening the link
in a browser. Those sites may see your IP address and may have their own privacy practices.
The developer does not receive these requests or their results.

## Sharing, backups, and files

Exporting a backup creates a file that you choose where to save or send (for example Files,
AirDrop, or email). Restoring a backup reads a file that you pick. The app does not send backups
anywhere on its own. Once you share a file, its handling is up to the app or person you shared it with.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage
data. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be posted in the app and in this file.
