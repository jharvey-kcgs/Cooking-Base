# Privacy Policy for Cooking Base

**Last updated: November 2026**

This is a starting draft, not a document reviewed by a lawyer — accurate
to how the app actually works today, but worth a second look before
treating it as final, especially if the app's data handling ever
changes.

## The short version

Cooking Base does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information about you. There is no account, no login, and no server of
ours in the middle. Your recipes live only on your own device. The only
time the app uses the internet is when you tell it to import a recipe
from a link you provide.

## What we don't do

- No account creation or login of any kind
- No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the app
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone
- No data is ever sold, rented, or shared with any third party for
  marketing purposes — there is no data collected to share in the
  first place

## What's stored on your device

Everything you create or choose in the app is saved locally in a database
on your device:

1. **Your recipes** — titles, categories, ingredients, instructions,
   notes, source links, tags, and favorites.
2. **Your preferences** — light or dark theme and your Metric / Standard
   units choice.
3. **Your recently viewed list** — which recipes you opened last, used
   only to show them on the Home screen.

None of this ever leaves your device on its own. There is no backend
server for the app to send it to, and no mechanism in the app that
transmits it anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## When the app uses the internet

Only when you ask it to import a recipe:

- **A recipe web link** — the app downloads the web page at the address
  you entered so it can read the recipe on it.
- **A Google Doc link** — the app downloads the text of the document at
  the link you entered. This works only for documents you've shared as
  "Anyone with the link". The app does not sign in to Google and does
  not access your Google account.

These are ordinary requests that go directly from your device to that
website or to Google, exactly like opening the link in a browser. The
site you're importing from can see your device's IP address and the page
request, the same way any website sees any visitor — this isn't something
Cooking Base adds, controls, or has access to. Those sites have their own
privacy practices. Nothing is sent to us, and we never see the links you
import or the recipes that come back.

## Backups and files

**Export backup** creates a file containing your recipes in the app's
temporary storage and opens your device's share sheet so *you* choose where
it goes (Files, AirDrop, email, and so on). **Restore from backup** reads a
file that you pick. The app never sends a backup anywhere on its own. Once
you save or share a backup file, how it's handled is up to the app or person
you gave it to.

## Children's privacy

Cooking Base does not knowingly collect any personal information from
anyone, including children. Since the app has no account system, no
data collection, and no way for anyone to submit personal information
through it at all, there is nothing to collect regardless of the age
of who's using it.

## Changes to this policy

If how this app handles data ever changes — for example, if a future
version adds a feature that does collect or transmit information — this
policy will be updated to reflect that honestly, with a new "Last
updated" date at the top, before any such change ships.
